Research Overview
Project Scope & Methodology
Establishing the foundation of event marketing excellence through dual-channel qualitative and quantitative research across Chennai event sectors.
Dual-Channel Research Methodology

📊 Research Methodology

This report employs a dual-channel research methodology to gather empirical data from diverse event sectors within the Chennai region.

• Primary Research: Direct on-site observation of visitor flow, stall placements, crowd density, and engagement dynamics across 2023 venues.
• Secondary Research: Analysis of official balance sheets, organizer reports (BAPASI / ITCOT), and historical press releases.

• Sector Coverage: B2B Industrial, Mass Cultural, and Institutional Educational.
• Evaluation Metrics: Visitor throughput, exhibitor ROI, budget allocation efficiency, and security protocols.
