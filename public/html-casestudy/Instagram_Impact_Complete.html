Research Scope & Methodology

🎯 Primary Study Scope

• Objective: Examine Instagram influencer impact on consumer purchase decisions & brand trust.
• Sample Size: 110 respondents selected via convenience sampling.
• Methodology: Primary quantitative Google Forms survey analyzed via percentile & Chi-Square testing.
• Demographics: 91.8% aged 18–25, 71.8% female, 89.1% students, 86.4% daily Instagram users.

4-Phase Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1 & 2: Selection & Pilot

• Phase 1 (Mo 1–2): Partner selection in Fashion (71.8%) focusing on macro-creators (10K–1M).
• Phase 2 (Mo 3–4): Pilot campaigns with 3–5 creators featuring mandatory #ad disclosures.

Phase 3 & 4: Scale & Expand

• Phase 3 (Mo 5–6): Scale top performers & launch UGC campaigns for neutral 40%.
• Phase 4 (Mo 6+): Expand to Food & Travel categories for cross-category growth.

Research Conclusion & Key Takeaways

Instagram influencer marketing is 70% more effective than traditional methods when executed with transparency, product quality (62.7%), macro-influencer partnerships (46.4%), and transparent labeling (86.4%). With 92.7% positive/neutral perception, authenticity is the ultimate competitive advantage.
