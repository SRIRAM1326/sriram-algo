Technical Infrastructure Highlights
REST API Layer

Flask Microservices

Modular blueprints for `/api/auth`, `/api/admin`, `/api/buyer`, and `/api/rep` with unified response wrappers.

ORM & Database

SQLAlchemy Normalized Schema

12 core relational tables enforce strict foreign key constraints between Buyers, Invoices, Reps, and Points Ledger.

Deployment

Docker & Coolify Ready

Standardized `Dockerfile`, `docker-compose.yml`, and `COOLIFY_DEPLOYMENT.md` setup for cloud deployment.

Authentication

Role-Based Security

JWT tokens store encrypted identity and role scope (`admin`, `buyer`, `rep`) to secure endpoint authorization.

Data Ingestion

Pandas & OpenPyXL Parsers

High-speed Excel/CSV stream reader normalizes diverse customer formats into standardized DB entities.

Mobile PWA

Offline & Geolocation

Service workers and Geolocation API provide field sales reps and buyers with resilient mobile performance.

Deep Dive Analysis
Pillar 1 — Admin ERP & Pillar 2 — Buyer B2B

Pillar 1 — Admin ERP Portal (`erp-prd`)

The central control hub designed for corporate executives, sales directors, and supply chain managers to govern products, pricing, rules, and data imports.

Management & Imports

Catalog & Bulk Import Engine

• Product Master & Tagging: Tag items as Normal, High-Margin (+300 pts), Old Stock (+500 pts), or Special.
• Bulk Ingestion Engine: Native parsers for `Customer Master.xlsx` and `Sales Invoice Register.xlsx`.
• Verification Hub: Review pending buyer registrations and approve customer codes.

Rules Engine & Audit

Config Versioning & Approvals

• Dynamic Rules Config: Set reward percentage (50% default), high spend threshold (₹2L), and tier parameters.
• Redemptions Ledger: Review, approve, or reject cash out payouts and product rewards.
• Audit Trail: Full system log tracking config changes, user activities, and point adjustments.

Pillar 2 — Buyer B2B Portal (`buyer-erp`)

A responsive mobile-first B2B ordering and loyalty hub enabling wholesale buyers to order inventory, track delivery status, and earn financial rewards.

Ordering & Scanner

Digital Catalog & Instant Claims

• B2B Product Ordering: Real-time stock visibility, category filtering, cart management, and order checkout.
• Instant QR Scanner: Camera-integrated scanner to claim bonus points instantly from invoice QR codes.
• Digital Statements: View order history, ledger balance, and downloadable invoice PDFs.

Loyalty & Gamification

Tiered Perks & Cash Redemptions

• Loyalty Tiers: Progress from Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum based on monthly purchase volume.
• Spin-the-Wheel: Daily interactive game awarding instant points based on probabilistic rules.
• Cash Out Engine: Convert 2,000 loyalty points into ₹200 cash credits or catalog gift rewards.
